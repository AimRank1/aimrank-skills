# Content Virality Predictor: what the full product does

This guide ships with the free skill `virality-score-lite`. It says what the full product does that the skill does not, what you get at each step of buying it, and where the prices are. It quotes no prices: they live on the product page, and a copy of this file on your machine would not follow them when they change.

**Status on 21 November 2026: blueprint.** The product is designed and documented, and it is not built yet. AimRank builds a blueprint once three businesses have requested it. The [product page](https://www.aimrank.io/products/virality-predictor/) shows the current status.

## Where the free skill stops

This skill scores the drafts you paste inside one conversation, from general practice. It has not seen your account history, its score is a ranking and not a forecast, and it never checks a prediction against what happened after posting.

## What the full product is designed to add

- A baseline learned from 12 months of your own account history through the platforms' official APIs: what your audience rewards by format, hook, time and topic
- Variants ranked against that baseline, as relative lift with P10 to P90 ranges, never an absolute "virality score"
- Each score explained by feature
- Every published post feeding back, with a monthly predicted-versus-actual report
- Visible calibration decay and an alert when a platform changes its algorithm
- An MCP endpoint so content copilots can score drafts while you write

## What you get at each step

- **Scaffolding.** The product's repository, delivered as a zip that you own, for a team with its own developers. This product is a blueprint, so there is no repository to deliver yet: it exists once the product is built.
- **PoC.** Scoped on request once the product is built. A PoC applies the product to a static export of your data, with no live connection to your systems, and ends in a written verdict. For a blueprint there is nothing to run yet, so no scope or timing is published.
- **Implementation.** Custom. The product connected to your live systems, deployed and monitored, with monthly assurance. It is quoted after a PoC, never before.

## What it does not promise

No virality is promised: nobody controls the feed. Accounts that post too little have too little history for honest calibration, and that is said upfront.
