# Reputation Guardian: what the full product does

This guide ships with the free skill `review-response-lite`. It says what the full product does that the skill does not, what you get at each step of buying it, and where the prices are. It quotes no prices: they live on the product page, and a copy of this file on your machine would not follow them when they change.

**Status on 21 November 2026: blueprint.** The product is designed and documented, and it is not built yet. AimRank builds a blueprint once three businesses have requested it. The [product page](https://www.aimrank.io/products/reputation-guardian/) shows the current status.

## Where the free skill stops

This skill drafts replies to the reviews you paste inside one conversation. It monitors no review sites, posts nothing, sends no review requests, keeps no record that replies and requests stayed compliant, and cannot remove a review.

## What the full product is designed to add

- One inbox for reviews from Google Business Profile, Facebook and industry sites, read in Lithuanian and English
- Aspect-based sentiment over time, so the recurring causes behind low ratings show up
- Draft replies checked for patient disclosure, prohibited health claims and defensive tone; a person approves and posts every reply
- Review requests sent to every patient after a visit, timed on appointment data and never filtered by how happy they seem
- A log of every reply's checks and every request, as the evidence that nothing was gated
- An MCP endpoint (analyze, draft_reply, evidence) for front-desk and marketing agents

## What you get at each step

- **Scaffolding.** The product's repository, delivered as a zip that you own, for a team with its own developers. This product is a blueprint, so there is no repository to deliver yet: it exists once the product is built.
- **PoC.** Scoped on request once the product is built. A PoC applies the product to a static export of your data, with no live connection to your systems, and ends in a written verdict. For a blueprint there is nothing to run yet, so no scope or timing is published.
- **Implementation.** Custom. The product connected to your live systems, deployed and monitored, with monthly assurance. It is quoted after a PoC, never before.

## Prices and how to ask

## What it does not promise

No negative review is removed and no rating is gamed: the rating moves only as the practice fixes what the reviews reveal. A person posts every reply.
