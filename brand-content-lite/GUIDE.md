# Brand-Voice Content + Compliance Filter: what the full product does

This guide ships with the free skill `brand-content-lite`. It says what the full product does that the skill does not, what you get at each step of buying it, and where the prices are. It quotes no prices: they live on the product page, and a copy of this file on your machine would not follow them when they change.

**Status on 21 November 2026: blueprint.** The product is designed and documented, and it is not built yet. AimRank builds a blueprint once three businesses have requested it. The [product page](https://www.aimrank.io/products/content-generation/) shows the current status.

## Where the free skill stops

This skill drafts and self-checks one piece at a time inside one conversation. It has not learned your voice from your approved content, its claims check is a reading and not a rule engine, it keeps no log of what was checked, and nothing it writes should be published without a person approving it.

## What the full product is designed to add

- Your brand book and advertising rules turned into an executable rule set: tone, terminology, banned claims, required disclosures
- Drafts in a voice tuned on your own best-performing content, shaped per channel
- A filter every draft passes before a person sees it, with violations rewritten or flagged with the rule that fired
- A log, per piece, of which rules it passed and what was rewritten
- Reviewer edits and misses feeding back as voice updates and new rules
- An MCP endpoint so the copilots your team already uses draft under the same guardrails

## What you get at each step

- **Scaffolding.** The product's repository, delivered as a zip that you own, for a team with its own developers. This product is a blueprint, so there is no repository to deliver yet: it exists once the product is built.
- **PoC.** Scoped on request once the product is built. A PoC applies the product to a static export of your data, with no live connection to your systems, and ends in a written verdict. For a blueprint there is nothing to run yet, so no scope or timing is published.
- **Implementation.** Custom. The product connected to your live systems, deployed and monitored, with monthly assurance. It is quoted after a PoC, never before.
