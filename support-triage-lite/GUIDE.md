# Lithuanian Support Triage + Draft Replies: what the full product does

This guide ships with the free skill `support-triage-lite`. It says what the full product does that the skill does not, what you get at each step of buying it, and where the prices are. It quotes no prices: they live on the product page, and a copy of this file on your machine would not follow them when they change.

**Status on 21 November 2026: blueprint.** The product is designed and documented, and it is not built yet. AimRank builds a blueprint once three businesses have requested it. The [product page](https://www.aimrank.io/products/support-triage-lt/) shows the current status.

## Where the free skill stops

This skill handles one ticket at a time inside one conversation. It is not connected to a helpdesk, routes nothing, grounds the draft only in the ticket itself (not in your policies or order data), keeps no record, and its confidence is agreement between two reads, not a calibrated probability.

## What the full product is designed to add

- A read-only helpdesk connection (Zendesk, Freshdesk, Intercom) with routing by intent, priority and SLA rules
- Draft replies grounded in your own policies and order data, with a citation behind each claim; a draft with no source is blocked
- Confidence that sends uncertain tickets to a person instead of guessing
- Accept-or-edit by your agents, with edits feeding back, and the covered share of tickets reported monthly
- PII redaction, an audit log, and an AI-assistance disclosure where EU AI Act Art. 50 requires one (in application since 2 August 2026)
- An MCP endpoint for support agents and copilots

## What you get at each step

- **Scaffolding.** The product's repository, delivered as a zip that you own, for a team with its own developers. This product is a blueprint, so there is no repository to deliver yet: it exists once the product is built.
- **PoC.** Scoped on request once the product is built. A PoC applies the product to a static export of your data, with no live connection to your systems, and ends in a written verdict. For a blueprint there is nothing to run yet, so no scope or timing is published.
- **Implementation.** Custom. The product connected to your live systems, deployed and monitored, with monthly assurance. It is quoted after a PoC, never before.

## What it does not promise

Out-of-policy questions (refund exceptions, legal threats, custom cases) are meant to be detected and routed to a person, not answered. A draft with no grounded source is never meant to reach a customer.
