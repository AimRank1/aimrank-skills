# Sales Call Analysis (LT): what the full product does

This guide ships with the free skill `call-analysis-lite`. It says what the full product does that the skill does not, what you get at each step of buying it, and where the prices are. It quotes no prices: they live on the product page, and a copy of this file on your machine would not follow them when they change.

**Status on 21 November 2026: blueprint.** The product is designed and documented, and it is not built yet. AimRank builds a blueprint once three businesses have requested it. The [product page](https://www.aimrank.io/products/sales-conversation-analysis-lt/) shows the current status.

## Where the free skill stops

This skill reads one transcript inside one conversation. It does not transcribe audio, has no benchmark built from your own winning calls, links nothing to a CRM, and keeps no consent record or history across calls.

## What the full product is designed to add

- Lithuanian speech recognition with your product vocabulary, speaker separation, and LT/EN/RU code-switching handled
- A consent-first recording flow, with PII redacted in transcripts and consent kept in the audit log
- Objections, pricing, competitors, questions and commitments tagged, each linked to its timestamp
- Coaching benchmarked against your own closed-won calls, not generic advice
- Summaries and next steps synced to your CRM
- An MCP endpoint for CRM and coaching copilots

## What you get at each step

- **Scaffolding.** The product's repository, delivered as a zip that you own, for a team with its own developers. This product is a blueprint, so there is no repository to deliver yet: it exists once the product is built.
- **PoC.** Scoped on request once the product is built. A PoC applies the product to a static export of your data, with no live connection to your systems, and ends in a written verdict. For a blueprint there is nothing to run yet, so no scope or timing is published.
- **Implementation.** Custom. The product connected to your live systems, deployed and monitored, with monthly assurance. It is quoted after a PoC, never before.

## What it does not promise

No scoring of personalities and no reading of emotions: only observable behaviour is counted. Nothing is analysed without recording consent.
