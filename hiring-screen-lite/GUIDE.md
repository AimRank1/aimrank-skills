# Hiring Screening with Bias Controls: what the full product does

This guide ships with the free skill `hiring-screen-lite`. It says what the full product does that the skill does not, what you get at each step of buying it, and where the prices are. It quotes no prices: they live on the product page, and a copy of this file on your machine would not follow them when they change.

**Status on 21 November 2026: blueprint.** The product is designed and documented, and it is not built yet. AimRank builds a blueprint once three businesses have requested it. The [product page](https://www.aimrank.io/products/hiring-screening/) shows the current status.

## Where the free skill stops

This skill drafts a shortlist from the CVs in one conversation. It does not blind the documents before reading them, measures no adverse impact across groups, keeps no decision log, and is not a compliant high-risk system. It never rejects anyone.

## What the full product is designed to add

- A blind pipeline: name, age, gender, photo and nationality masked before scoring
- Criteria designed with your recruiters and documented as job-related, with every score traced to evidence in the CV
- Borderline candidates routed to a person; no automatic rejection, ever
- Adverse-impact (four-fifths rule) monitoring on real outcomes, with recruiter overrides and drift on the candidate mix watched
- A full decision log and a candidate transparency note
- A map of the Annex III obligations and the documentation they call for (those obligations apply from 2 December 2027)
- An MCP endpoint for recruiting copilots, under the same controls

## What you get at each step

- **Scaffolding.** The product's repository, delivered as a zip that you own, for a team with its own developers. This product is a blueprint, so there is no repository to deliver yet: it exists once the product is built.
- **PoC.** Scoped on request once the product is built. A PoC applies the product to a static export of your data, with no live connection to your systems, and ends in a written verdict. For a blueprint there is nothing to run yet, so no scope or timing is published.
- **Implementation.** Custom. The product connected to your live systems, deployed and monitored, with monthly assurance. It is quoted after a PoC, never before.

## What it does not promise

No automatic rejection and no promise of "bias-free": the commitment is measured, monitored and documented, with a person deciding on every candidate.
