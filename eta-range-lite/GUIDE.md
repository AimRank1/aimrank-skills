# Cross-Border Logistics ETA: what the full product does

This guide ships with the free skill `eta-range-lite`. It says what the full product does that the skill does not, what you get at each step of buying it, and where the prices are. It quotes no prices: they live on the product page, and a copy of this file on your machine would not follow them when they change.

**Status on 21 November 2026: blueprint.** The product is designed and documented, and it is not built yet. AimRank builds a blueprint once three businesses have requested it. The [product page](https://www.aimrank.io/products/logistics-eta/) shows the current status.

## Where the free skill stops

This skill estimates from the shipment details you give it inside one conversation. Its range is a judgment and not learned from your shipment history, it has no live border or carrier data, and it tracks nothing after the estimate.

## What the full product is designed to add

- Lane models learned from your own transport-management and telematics history, per segment, carrier and weekday
- Customs queues from live and historical data, driver-rest rules (Regulation (EC) No 561/2006) and ferry slots
- A P10 to P90 window at booking that narrows as milestones pass
- Calibration shown to you monthly: how often reality landed inside the promised window
- Proactive customer notifications when the risk shifts
- Every ETA logged with its inputs, and an MCP endpoint for your portal and operations copilots

## What you get at each step

- **Scaffolding.** The product's repository, delivered as a zip that you own, for a team with its own developers. This product is a blueprint, so there is no repository to deliver yet: it exists once the product is built.
- **PoC.** Scoped on request once the product is built. A PoC applies the product to a static export of your data, with no live connection to your systems, and ends in a written verdict. For a blueprint there is nothing to run yet, so no scope or timing is published.
- **Implementation.** Custom. The product connected to your live systems, deployed and monitored, with monthly assurance. It is quoted after a PoC, never before.

## What it does not promise

A range, not a single hour. Customs queues are modelled, but a surprise inspection is still a surprise: the aim is to tell the customer earlier, not to pretend it cannot happen.
